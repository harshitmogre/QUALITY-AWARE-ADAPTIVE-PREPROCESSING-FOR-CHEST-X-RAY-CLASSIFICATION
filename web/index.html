04 / INTERPRET WITH CARE

What this pilot cannot tell us.

A research prototype, not a medical device.
More validation is needed.
01

Image-level split

Patient identifiers are unavailable. The same person's images may appear across train and test sets.

02

Mixed data sources

Classes come from different source collections, so a model may learn source-specific patterns.

03

One model, one seed

The current expanded comparison uses one EfficientNet-B0 run per configuration. DenseNet and repeated-seed comparisons remain to be run.

04

No clinical validation

Simple image statistics and synthetic corruptions do not establish diagnostic usefulness or safety.
